FIFA World Cup 2030

FIFA World Cup 2030TM will be the 24th edition of the men's global football tournament, primarily hosted by Morocco, Spain and Portugal across June and July 2030

Host Countries

The FIFA World Cup 2030TM will be primarily co-hosted by Morocco, Spain and Portugal, with Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay each one hosting one match to comemorate the 100-year anniversary of the first World Cup.
All six co-hosting nations (Morocco, Portugal, Spain, Argentina, Paraguay, and Uruguay) will receive automatic qualification.

Tournament Planning and Teams

48 teams will take part in the tournament and there will be a total of 104 matches (101 matches hosted by the three main co-hosts and 3 matches hosted by Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay). Inside these 48 teams, there will be teams from every continent, this means that every FIFA confederation will have at least one country participating.

Stadiums

The event will be played in 23 stadiums, with 11 stadiums being in Spain, 6 in Morocco, 3 in Portugal, 1 in Uruguay, 1 in Argentina and 1 in Paraguay.
The opening games in the tournament will be played in Uruguay, Argentina and Paraguay, while the rest will be played in the stadiums located inside of the three main co-hosters.
